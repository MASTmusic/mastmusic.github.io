Biography

Tim Conley / MAST

A Philadelphia native based in Los Angeles, Tim Conley is a jazz musician, composer, producer and electronic musician. His solo project MAST draws together distinct sonic elements and channels them through intense live performance.

The latest MAST release, Battle Hymns of the Republic, is a deliberate reflection on events in the United States and around the world. It is a spiritual protest against misinformation and fascist ideologies, scored by orchestral arrangements, spoken word, jazz sensibilities, angular electronic beats and samples.

In 2018, MAST released Thelonious Sphere Monk, a 16-track cosmic journey through the Thelonious Monk songbook. The album features Chris Speed, Brian Marsella, Makaya McCraven, Jason Fraticelli, Dan Rosenboom and others.

Love and War_ was released in 2016 through Alpha Pup Records. The conceptual three-act album features Taylor McFerrin, Louis Cole, Tim Lefebvre, Makaya McCraven, the Fresh Cut Orchestra, Andrée Belle and more.

The 2014 debut Omni features Anna Wise, Jeremiah Jae and Low Leaf, followed by the conceptual EPs Omniverse; Verse 1 and Verse 2.

Conley received a Jazz Residency Grant from the Kimmel Center to commission Life Mosaic, an electronic, classical and jazz hybrid performed with the 11-piece Fresh Cut Orchestra.

He has performed with José James, Mark Guiliana, Taylor McFerrin, Tim Lefebvre, Louis Cole and Makaya McCraven. Since 2022, he has toured on guitar and keyboards with Trevor Hall, including a 2024 performance with the Colorado Symphony at Red Rocks Amphitheatre.
