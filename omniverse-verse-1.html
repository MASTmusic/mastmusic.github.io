About the work

MAST releases the first in a new series of EPs titled “Omniverse; Verse 1” on Alpha Pup Records, following the highly acclaimed release of his 2014 debut LP “Omni,” which featured Anna Wise (Sonnymoon, Kendrick Lamar), Jeremiah Jae (Brainfeeder/Warp), RYAT (Brainfeeder) and Low Leaf.

The “Omniverse” series unfolds through multiple EPs, or “Verses,” released via Alpha Pup Records. Each Verse is conceptually, thematically and sonically connected, creating one massive through-composed sonic universe—or, in this case, a compositional “Omniverse.”

“Verse 1” features swirling guitars, dynamic keyboards and pulsating beats, all performed by Tim Conley aka MAST in his Los Angeles-based home studio. Released in September, Verse 1 explores themes of autumn and the astral bodies that create it. The six tracks bridge into one another, forming a cohesive mix inspired by the cosmos.
