On stage

Performance

MAST channels composition and improvisation through intense, responsive live sets.

Improvisation as architecture

With a composer’s mind and a jazz musician’s desire to explore new realms, MAST has performed throughout the United States, Canada, Europe and Japan.

Tim Conley has appeared at Low End Theory, the Kennedy Center, Lincoln Center, Winter Jazzfest, the London Jazz Festival, Halifax Jazz Festival, Angel City Jazz Festival and Gilles Peterson’s Worldwide Showcase in Tokyo.
