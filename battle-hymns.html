About the work

MAST’s Battle Hymns of the Republic is a highly imaginative dissident’s brew addressing urgent environmental and socio-political issues. Moving orchestral arrangements, poignant spoken word, impassioned jazz language, angular electronic beats and samples reflect on present-day battles for unity, equality, facts, justice and empathy.

Each track—or “hymn”—considers attacks on who and what the United States claims to be, while asking what responsibility we hold toward one another and the wider global community. The album is a spiritual protest against misinformation, fascist ideologies, institutional racism and denial of climate change.

Dwight Trible layers proverbs, quotations and original writing throughout “Benevolence.” Jimetta Rose contributes the exploratory “All of the Colors,” a deep examination of race in America. Andrée Belle appears on “The Golden Door,” pairing Emma Lazarus’s 1883 poem with original text.
