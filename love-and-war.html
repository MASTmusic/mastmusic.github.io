About the work

Structured as a three-act play, Love and War_ moves through orchestral writing, electronic production, improvisation and song form. The album was released by Alpha Pup Records in October 2016.

Featuring

Taylor McFerrin · Louis Cole · Tim Lefebvre · Makaya McCraven · Fresh Cut Orchestra · Andrée Belle · and more
